# Community conduct

VGET welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Be respectful, critique ideas and evidence rather than people, credit others' work, and help newcomers participate. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing and disclosure of private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other spaces managed by this project. Maintainer @HaotianG handles reports and may edit or remove violating content, limit participation, or block accounts according to severity and repeated behavior. Explain moderation decisions where practical without exposing private information.

Use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for sensitive conduct reports. For non-sensitive moderation questions, open an issue without publishing another person's private information. Do not use the security advisory channel for ordinary conduct disputes.
